The game

An observation game that makes you smarter

ArtClue blends a narrative mystery, hidden objects and trivia. Each case takes just a few minutes, and every clue teaches you something new.

🔍

Clues hidden in the artwork

One riddle, one painting: it's up to you to tap the right detail. Zoom, pinch, double-tap, and explore every corner like a real expert.

🕵️

A real mystery

A mysterious mastermind is challenging you. Every solved case gives you a letter; put them together to unmask the culprit.

💡

350 fun facts

Why isn't The Night Watch a night scene? Where does the word “Impressionism” come from? Every clue reveals a secret.

🎨

The Forger's Workshop

Spot the difference, museum edition: compare the original and the fake, and find the 5 mistakes before the forger gets away.

📖

The Book of Secrets

Your painting collection, a timeline from the Renaissance to the 19th century, and every clue you've found, ready to revisit anytime.

🌍

Atlas & passport

Where each work was painted and where it hangs today. Stamp your passport, country by country.

How to play

Three moves, centuries of secrets

01

Read the riddle

“A witness signed the wall, the way you'd carve your name.” Where should you look?

02

Tap the detail

Zoom in, search, tap. A wrong lead costs a little instinct; a hint helps if you're stuck.

03

Uncover the secret

A close-up, the explanation, a fun fact. Then the final deduction to close the case.
