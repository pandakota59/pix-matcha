App: ArtClue: Art History Mysteries (Android, package com.pandakotah.artclue)
Last updated: November 19, 2026 · Version 1.1

In short. ArtClue requires no account and collects no personal data for its own use. Your progress is stored only on your device. The app is funded by ads served by Google AdMob. In Europe, a consent screen lets you accept or decline personalized ads, and you can change your mind at any time from within the app.

1. Data controller

The ArtClue app is published by Pix & Matcha, an independent studio based in Lille, France (“we”).
Contact for any question about your data: pixandmatcha@gmail.com.

2. Data we process ourselves

None. ArtClue does not ask for an account, email address, name or location, and does not access your contacts, photos, microphone or camera. The app contains no analytics and sends no data to our servers: we don't have any.

Game progress. Solved cases, clues found, scores and best times are stored locally on your device (app storage). This data is never sent to us. You can erase it at any time from Privacy & credits → Erase my progress, or by uninstalling the app. If Android backup is enabled on your device, this progress may be included in your Google account's encrypted backup, under your sole control.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To stay free, the app shows ads (interstitials between cases and optional rewarded videos) provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). To serve and measure these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process:

  • your Android advertising ID (which you can reset in your device settings);
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • technical information about your device (model, Android version, language, carrier);
  • ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) and diagnostic data.

Legal basis. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, personalized advertising relies on your consent, collected at first launch through Google's consent screen (an IAB TCF–certified consent management platform). If you decline, only non-personalized (contextual) ads may be shown, based on the publisher's legitimate interest in funding the app.

Withdrawing or changing your consent. At any time: title screen → Privacy & credits → Manage my ad choices. You can also reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

Google acts as an independent controller for this data. Learn more: How Google uses data and the Google Privacy Policy. Data may be transferred outside the European Union (notably to the United States) under the standard contractual clauses and the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework to which Google adheres.

U.S. users. We do not sell your personal information. Ad personalization by Google can be limited from your device settings, as described above.

5. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, as well as the right to withdraw your consent at any time. Since we hold no data about you, these rights are exercised:

  • for game progress: directly in the app (erase);
  • for advertising data: with Google, through My Ad Center or your device settings.

For any request or question: pixandmatcha@gmail.com. You may also file a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (www.cnil.fr).

6. Children

ArtClue is a cultural game for a general audience. It is not specifically directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data about them. Some of the artworks include artistic nudity or dramatic historical scenes, consistent with the age rating shown on the Play Store.

7. Security

The app does not use any network connection for its own purposes. The only network communications are those of the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which are encrypted (HTTPS). Game resources (images, fonts) are bundled in the app.

8. Changes

Any change to this policy will be published at this address with its update date. In case of a substantial change (new data collection, new partner), the app will let you know.
